01 · Who we are

One foundation.
Six paths to change.

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is based in Chhattisgarh and works across India. Its initiatives combine immediate community support with education, skills, dignity, and environmental responsibility.

The foundation reports Section 8 registration, 80G and 12A certification, CSR-1 registration, NGO Darpan–NITI Aayog registration, and IAF ISO 9001:2015 certification.

04 · A closer look

Creative learning can make participation feel possible.

The supplied field photograph shows children presenting Republic Day artwork alongside volunteers. It turns a broad goal—education for every child—into a visible, community-based learning moment.

Field story · Project Bachpanshala
01

The need

Children in underserved communities may have fewer opportunities for structured, creative, and supportive learning outside formal classrooms.

02

The response

Volunteers can use art, civic themes, workshops, and mentoring to create engaging learning experiences and safe spaces for expression.

03

The intended value

These activities support participation, confidence, curiosity, and a positive connection with education.
